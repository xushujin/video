QingYing is a lightweight, fully offline video player for the files that are
already on your phone. No account, no network, no ads, no runtime permissions.

<b>Library</b>
* Add a local folder with the system file picker. No storage permission is
  requested - the folder grant is persisted by the system (SAF)
* Each folder becomes a group, and the group is named after the folder itself
* Recursive scanning, up to 8 levels deep and 3000 files, hidden files skipped
* Files are ordered naturally, so episode 2 comes before episode 10
* 21 container formats are recognised: mp4, m4v, mkv, webm, avi, mov, flv, ts,
  m2ts, mts, 3gp, 3g2, wmv, asf, rmvb, rm, mpg, mpeg, vob, ogv and f4v
* Duration and resolution are probed in the background when a group is expanded,
  so adding a folder is never slowed down by it
* Thumbnails come from the frame at 1 second - frame 0 is usually black

<b>Playback</b>
* The player runs in a foreground service, not in the screen you are looking at,
  so audio keeps going in the background and with the screen off
* When you leave the player screen or switch away, the video surface is detached
  and the video track is disabled, so only audio is decoded. It saves battery,
  and avoids drawing to a surface that has already been destroyed
* Sleep timer: presets from 5 to 120 minutes, a custom number of minutes, or
  "when this video ends". At zero it either pauses playback or exits the app
* Speed from 0.5x to 3x, drag to seek, or jump 15 seconds back and forward
* A-B repeat: mark a start and an end point to loop that segment, with the range
  drawn under the progress bar
* Previous and next move through the queue, which is the folder you opened
* Per-video resume positions, plus a "Continue watching" row on the home screen
* Portrait and landscape layouts; landscape hides the system bars for a full
  screen picture
* A manual landscape switch in the player controls, for when auto-rotate is off
* Swipe gestures on the full-screen player: left half adjusts the volume, right
  half adjusts the brightness, with a percentage indicator
* Swipe left or right to seek; the picture follows your finger and the offset and
  target time are shown while you drag. How far a full swipe goes (30 seconds to
  5 minutes) is configurable in the settings

<b>Screen</b>
* The screen stays on while the player screen is in the foreground, so a video
  you are watching is never interrupted by the screen timeout
* Switching away releases it immediately, so listening to audio in the
  background does not leave the screen lit all night

<b>Privacy</b>
* No runtime permissions are requested at all
* The notification permission is declared but never requested. Without it the
  media notification is simply not shown - background playback is unaffected
* Video folders are chosen through the system file picker and can be removed
  from the list at any time
* Everything stays on your device

<b>Notes</b>
* Whether an exotic codec plays depends on your device's decoders. Files that
  cannot be played report the actual error instead of silently showing black
* If only the audio track cannot be decoded (common with MP2 audio inside AVI),
  playback continues muted instead of stopping, and you are told once
* RealMedia (.rmvb) is not supported at all - the container has no extractor and
  phones have no RealVideo decoder, so those files need converting
* Picture-in-picture is not implemented
* The F-Droid build is signed with F-Droid's own key, so it cannot upgrade a
  copy installed from GitHub Releases - uninstall that copy first
