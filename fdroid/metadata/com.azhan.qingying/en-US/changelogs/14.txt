Audio track selection, a cleaner seek gesture, and a screen lock.

* Files with several audio tracks (dubbed, original, commentary) now show an
  Audio button in the top bar; pick a track, or go back to automatic
* The choice is dropped when the next video starts, so it never points at a track
  that does not exist there
* Swiping left or right no longer draws the time overlay in the middle of the
  screen - the picture itself follows your finger
* A lock button in full screen disables every touch: volume, brightness, seeking
  and the controls. Tap the "locked" pill in the bottom-left corner to unlock;
  rotating back to portrait unlocks as well
