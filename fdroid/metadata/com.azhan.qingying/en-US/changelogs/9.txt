Seeking by swiping now follows your finger, and its speed is configurable.

* The picture jumps while you drag (throttled to about five seeks per second), and
  the exact position is applied when you lift your finger
* New setting: Settings - Playback - Swipe seek distance, choosing how much a full
  screen swipe covers: 30 seconds, 1, 2 or 5 minutes (2 minutes by default)
* Short videos still shrink the range so a single swipe cannot jump to the end
* Throttling matters: seeking on every frame makes the decoder rebuild constantly
  and the video stutters instead
