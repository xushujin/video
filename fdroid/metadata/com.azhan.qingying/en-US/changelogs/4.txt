Added A-B repeat: pick a segment and loop it.

* Mark the start with A and the end with B while the video plays; the loop starts
  as soon as both points are set
* Marking only B loops from the beginning of the video to that point
* A bar under the progress bar shows the looping segment, and the top bar shows
  when a loop is active
* The A-B panel shows both times (tap one to jump there), a loop switch, and a
  way to clear the points. It works in portrait and landscape
* The loop belongs to the current video only and is cleared when the next episode
  starts; it is not saved to your resume positions
