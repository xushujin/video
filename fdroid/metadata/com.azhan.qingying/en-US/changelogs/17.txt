Clearer messages for files that still cannot play.

* Picking a .rm or .rmvb file now says up front that RealMedia is not supported,
  with the ffmpeg command to convert it, instead of failing after a prepare
* The audio track panel lists which codecs the built-in software decoder covers
* The "no playable audio" notice now explains that MP2, WMA and RealAudio are
  outside that list and need converting to AAC
