Videos whose audio track cannot be decoded now play muted instead of failing.

* Some AVI files carry MPEG-1 Layer II audio, which Android reports as supported
  but then fails to decode - previously the whole video refused to play. Now the
  audio track is disabled and the picture keeps going, with a short notice
* The fallback applies to that one video only; the next episode gets its audio back
* Error messages now say what actually broke - audio decoding, video decoding,
  an unsupported container, or an unreadable file - and suggest what to do about it
* RealMedia (.rmvb) still cannot be played: there is no extractor for that
  container and phones have no RealVideo decoder. Convert those files first
