Added full-screen swipe gestures.

* Swipe up or down on the left half of the player to change the volume
* Swipe up or down on the right half to change the screen brightness
* A percentage indicator appears in the middle and fades out shortly after you
  stop moving your finger
* Brightness only affects the player window, so the system setting is left alone
  and no permission is needed; it is handed back when you leave the player
* Dragging the progress bar or the panels still takes priority over the gestures
