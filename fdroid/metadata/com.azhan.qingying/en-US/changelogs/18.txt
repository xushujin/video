MP2 audio now plays, and the player got smaller.

* The bundled FFmpeg is now built for this app only, with the audio decoders it
  needs - including MP2 (MPEG-1 Layer II), the codec found in many old AVI files
* AC3, EAC3, DTS, TrueHD, AAC, MP3, FLAC, Opus, Vorbis and ALAC all decode in
  software, so a device codec can no longer be the reason a file stays silent
* Video is still decoded in hardware; only audio goes through FFmpeg
* Because the build leaves out video decoders, the APK shrank from 9.1 MB to 6.9 MB
