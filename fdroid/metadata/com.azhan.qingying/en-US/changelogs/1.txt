First release.

* Add a local folder and it becomes a group named after that folder
* 21 container formats, natural file ordering, background duration probing
* Background audio: the player runs in a foreground service, and the video track
  is disabled when you leave the player screen
* Sleep timer with presets, a custom duration, or "when this video ends"
* Portrait and landscape layouts, landscape is full screen
* Screen stays on only while the player screen is in the foreground
* Per-video resume positions and a continue-watching row
* Playback speed from 0.5x to 3x, 15 second seek jumps
* No runtime permissions are requested
