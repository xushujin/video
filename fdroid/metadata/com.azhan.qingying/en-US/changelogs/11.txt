Audio decoding now falls back to a software decoder before giving up.

* When the audio track fails to decode, the player retries once with the system's
  software decoder instead of muting the video straight away
* Only if that also fails is the audio track disabled and the picture continues
* The decoder name is now included in the error details, so a broken device codec
  is easy to identify
* Software decoding is limited to audio; video stays on hardware, since software
  4K decoding would drop frames
