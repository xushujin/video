Added horizontal swipes to seek.

* Drag left or right on the video to jump backwards or forwards; a full screen
  width covers two minutes, or the whole video if it is shorter
* The indicator in the middle shows the offset (+0:15) and where you will land
  (12:34 / 45:00)
* The jump happens when you lift your finger - seeking on every move makes the
  decoder rebuild and stutters
* Horizontal and vertical swipes are told apart by direction, so volume and
  brightness swipes still work as before
